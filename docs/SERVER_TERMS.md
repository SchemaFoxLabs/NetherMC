## Current operation and possible future support

The server currently does not sell cosmetics, permissions, or other paid content, and does not accept sponsorships, donations, or payments. In the future, we may consider allowing players with authenticated Minecraft accounts to contribute directly toward actual server hosting costs. Any such arrangement would be announced separately before it becomes available.

Future eligibility would relate to an authenticated Minecraft account's UUID/IGN, without requiring public disclosure of real-world identity. No such channel is currently open. Any actual arrangement will be announced separately with regard to the rules and relevant licenses in force at that time.

Support Rank and player-report rewards are non-purchase contribution rewards, unrelated to financial contributions. Eligibility must be verified; submission or acceptance does not automatically establish it.

## Player transactions

The operating team does not provide escrow, settlement, or fund-recovery services for player transactions. This does not exclude obligations that cannot be excluded by applicable law. Prohibited commercial trading, fiat or virtual currency transactions, and using FSPoint as currency within the server and officially managed communities are addressed under the server rules.

## Handling capacity and material inquiries

Staff review submissions as time and available evidence allow. Some reports may remain pending for an extended period.

The usual 1–7 days, or 8–30 days during busy periods, are reference estimates for initial handling or a first reply only and may be exceeded, especially for feature requests. They are not deadlines for investigation, resolution, or sanctions.

For consultation before submitting supplementary material only: `mc-contact@schemafoxlabs.com`. Ask about a suitable submission method before sending private account details or sensitive evidence. The mailbox may be unable to receive attachments larger than **20 MiB**; first ask in a text-only message about larger material.

Submit only redacted material in public Issues.
