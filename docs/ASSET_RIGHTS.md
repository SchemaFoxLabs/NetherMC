## Copyright and licensing contact

Contact: `compliance@schemafoxlabs.com`. Identify the relevant work, the specific location of its use, the basis of your rights, and your request.

The mailbox may be unable to receive attachments larger than **20 MiB**; ask in a text-only message about submitting larger material first.

Claims based on a license change should identify the license terms alleged to apply at the time the server obtained or first used the asset. Where we possess contemporaneous records of an earlier license grant, those records will be considered during review.

For fabricated licensing evidence, knowingly false rights claims, or malicious abuse of removal procedures, the operator reserves the right to seek remedies under applicable law. This statement does not prejudge a particular complaint as unlawful.
