## Can’t do list

The community standard is simple: participate in good faith, respect other players, protect privacy, and use public channels for gameplay and community coordination.

Do not:

- Use hate speech or aggressive and insulting language toward other players.
- Repeatedly harass, threaten, blackmail, or expose another person’s private information.
- Impersonate staff or another player.
- Scam, phish, distribute malicious links, spam, advertise, or post unauthorized external media links.
- Post sexual innuendo, pornography, R18G content, CSAM, or a nickname or skin that violates the content rules.
- Use public or network-wide chat for political campaigning, calls to political action, propaganda, repeated political slogans, or sustained disruptive political debate.
- Organize harmful cult, terrorist, or fascist activity, including harmful recruitment, coercion, or advocacy of violence.
- Attack the server, abuse an exploit, use prohibited bot automation, cheat, boost Ranked scores, or snipe players.
- Conduct prohibited commercial activity or trade fiat or virtual currency through NetherMC services.
- Use FSPoint as currency, payment, exchange value, or an off-server trading instrument.
- Evade a ban, mute, or other restriction.
- Fabricate evidence, deliberately misrepresent events, misuse another player’s report record, or abuse reports and appeals.

## Restriction types

| Type | Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Join Block** | Prevents entry to the entire NetherMC network |
| **Feature Block** | Restricts a feature such as network-wide chat or Ranked |
| **SubServer Block** | Prevents entry to a specified subserver; other subservers are not automatically restricted |

## Violations and penalties

### Chat and content

| Violation | Penalty |
| :--- | :--- |
| Aggressive or insulting statements | Network-wide chat Feature Block: **1 minute** for the first occurrence, **indefinite** for the second |
| Advertising or marketing, unauthorized external media links, sexual innuendo, pornography, or R18G | Network-wide chat Feature Block: **1 day**, then **7 days**, then **indefinite** |
| Political campaigning, calls to political action, propaganda, repeated political slogans, or sustained disruptive political debate in public or network-wide chat | Network-wide chat Feature Block: **1 day**, then **7 days**, then **indefinite** |
| Nickname or skin content violation | Join Block for approximately **1 day**; skins can only be reported in-game |

### Cheating and competitive integrity

| Violation | Penalty |
| :--- | :--- |
| Anti-cheat detection | Fixed **15-day** SubServer Block on the affected subserver |
| Cheating confirmed by staff | SubServer Block on the affected subserver: **7 / 30 / 180 / 360 days**, remaining at **360 days** thereafter |
| Ranked score boosting | Ranked Feature Block until the season ends or indefinitely |
| Solo Unranked sniping | SubServer Block on the affected subserver for at least **1 day**, up to long-term or permanent |
| Solo Ranked sniping | SubServer Block on the affected subserver for at least **30 days**, up to long-term or permanent |
| Coordinated sniping | **Permanent Join Block** for every verified participant |

The staff-confirmed cheating schedule excludes Autoclicker at or below 20 CPS and Sprint. This exception applies only to that manual schedule and does not override anti-cheat enforcement or other rules.

Solo sniping may also result in a Ranked Feature Block for the rest of the season or indefinitely. Ranked penalties do not change the normal seasonal FSPoint refresh.

Staff verify coordinated sniping from relevant game records, including matchmaking frequency, timing overlap, recurring accounts, participant counts, matches, and behavior. Community feedback may provide leads. Public allegations, repost counts, votes, one coincidental match, or a single statistical anomaly do not establish participation. NetherMC has no player-created organization system, so guild, organization, or fixed-team labels do not establish involvement.

### Indefinite network restrictions

The following violations receive an **indefinite Join Block**:

- Hate speech.
- Harmful cult, terrorist, or fascist activity.
- Server attacks, including ServerCrasher.
- Prohibited bot automation, including automated Ranked play.
- CSAM.
- Prohibited commercial activity or fiat or virtual currency trading.
- Using FSPoint as currency, payment, exchange value, or an off-server trading instrument.
- Scams, phishing, or malicious links.
- Repeated harassment, threats, or blackmail.
- Sharing or threatening to share another person’s private information.
- Evading bans or mutes.

## Review and appeals

Staff determine penalties from the applicable rule and the available evidence, including duration, scale, repetition, participation, and impact. A reporter’s confidence, report count, or reward eligibility does not determine a penalty.

Reasonable criticism, good-faith reports, and ordinary appeals are not violations. Insufficient evidence may result in no penalty or a request for more information.

Submit appeals through [NetherMC-PublicReport](https://github.com/Labs-mcdev/NetherMC-PublicReport/). Indefinite restrictions have no scheduled end. Permanent restrictions do not expire automatically, but mistaken decisions may be appealed. Repeated appeals without new facts or evidence may be combined or closed.

## Rule changes

New or increased penalties generally apply to later conduct. Ongoing violations or current safety risks may require an immediate protective restriction with a recorded reason and an appeal route.
