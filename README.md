## About the project

NetherMC is a competitive Minecraft server project focused on delivering a modern PvP experience for Asian players.

Rebuilt and maintained by Schema'Fox Labs, NetherMC combines the classic 1.8 PvP mechanics with new technologies, optimized infrastructure, and creative gameplay designs.

Our goal is to preserve old-school Minecraft while introducing new experiences for modern players.


## Feedback and issue tracking

Read the [feedback rules](docs/FeedbackRule_QA-SI.md) before submitting a bug report, question, or feature request.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Describe a concrete issue or need clearly. Unsupported subjective assessments and reports without substantive details are not accepted.

> [!WARNING]
> Check that your report accurately describes what you observed.
>
> Public issues are visible to others. Remove real names, personal contact details, identifying information, and real-world location information from text, logs, and screenshots. Published information may not be fully retractable.

Staff review submissions as time and available evidence allow. Some reports may remain pending for an extended period.

The usual 1–7 days, or 8–30 days during busy periods, are reference estimates for initial handling or a first reply only and may be exceeded, especially for feature requests. They are not deadlines for investigation, resolution, or sanctions.

Feature requests may take longer while staff assess necessity, feasibility, development scheduling, and compatibility with the current architecture.

Report ordinary functional bugs, including severe ones without security implications, through the Bug report form. Report vulnerabilities or exploitable issues through the Security page linked above.

For player reports while staff are offline or appeals of mistaken Join Blocks, Feature Blocks, or SubServer Blocks, use [NetherMC-PublicReport](https://github.com/Labs-mcdev/NetherMC-PublicReport/).

Switch servers:

```minecraft-command
/server <identification>
```

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Practice | `practice` | A modern Practice PvP of 1v1,Events and PartyPvP | (Core 1.8x)1.7.10 ~ 1.20x |
| Bridge | `bridge` | Bridging,Fighting and practice blocks use | (Core 1.8x)1.7.10 ~ 1.20x |

| PracticeX | soon | Practice, but 1.20x | (Core 1.20x)1.7.10 ~ 1.20x |

## Supporter rewards

Verified serious vulnerability, bug, or security reports that meet the reward criteria will earn you:

- A Support Rank with long-term validity.
- Public contributor recognition (which may move to a separate document if the list becomes too long).

Your rank will be upgraded based on the value and number of your reports:

**Support → SupportX → BugFinder → IssueExpert**

Eligibility is verified by staff. Submission or acceptance alone does not earn a reward. These are non-purchase contribution rewards and are not tied to future financial contributions.
